## Sections (live order)
1. Breadcrumb: Home > Services > Services Directory > Request Occupational Radiation Monitoring service (current).
   Links: en/home, en/services, en/services/services-directory.
2. Title row: H1 "Request Occupational Radiation Monitoring service" + 3 icon buttons: Share (addtoany link), Print (javascript), Download (PDF handler, id=29348).
3. Intro: "This service allows private and governmental facilities to monitor the occupational radiation doses for workers in the radiation field on a regular basis."
4. Service Process (7 numbered steps, vertical):
   1. Submit the service requests via e-mail
   2. Fill out the forms sent for the service request and send them via email.
   3. Complete the electronic payment process (if available) through the sent online link and send the payment receipt via email
   4. Receive an email with the appointment for collection the radiation dosimeters from the center for the first-time.
   5. Receive an email with the appointment for the upcoming radiation dosimeters collection and return the used radiation dosimeters to the center within one working week from reciving the new dosimeters.
   6. Receive a report on the radiation dosimeters readings for the employees via email.
   7. Receive a payment link to pay the annual fees, if applicable, to renew the service.
5. Conditions & Requirements (2 bullets):
   - The prescribed fees must be paid as per the decree of the Council of Ministers (No. 3 for year 2001) for private sector.
   - Approval of the Radiation Protection Center at the Emirates Health Services before the service.
6. FAQ's (accordion, 1 item, shown expanded):
   - Q: What is the importance of conducting periodic occupational radiation monitoring?
   - A: This service is important for measuring the Radiation doses to workers in the Radiation field
7. Info rows (icon + label left, value right, divider between), in order:
   - Service completion duration: 5 Working Days
   - Service Fees: "Governmental facilities: Free"; "The fees for the private sector:" then 6 bullets:
     - Quarterly reports: 300 AED per person per year is charged for workers, for whole-body or extremity radiation dosimeters.
     - Reports every two months: 400 AED per person per year is charged for workers, for whole-body or extremity radiation dosimeters.
     - Monthly reports: 700 AED per person per year is charged for workers, for whole-body or extremity radiation dosimeters.
     - Quarterly reports: 600 AED per person per year is charged for workers, for neutron and mixed radiation dosimeters.
     - Bi-monthly reports: 800 AED per person per year is charged for workers, for neutron and mixed radiation dosimeters.
     - Monthly reports: 1,400 AED per person per year is charged for workers, for neutron and mixed radiation dosimeters.
   - Service channels: Service Delivery Centers; Email
   - Target audience: For workers in the field of Radiation in government and private facilities
   - Service Limitations: This service is provided to radiation workers in different facilities
   - Service Provision Timings: "Radiation Protection Center, working hours as follows:" Monday to Thursday (7:30-15:00); Friday (7:30 - 11:30)
   - Service Type: Transactional
   - Sub Service Type: Variation
8. Sidebar (right, grey panel): Required Documents (5 bullets):
   - Official letter from the facility to request the service for first time.
   - Establishment decree for governmental facilities.
   - Application of service request form.
   - Facility information form
   - Additional documents may be requested by the Radiation Protection Center depending on the type of service.
